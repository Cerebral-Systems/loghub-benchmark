# Label provenance & known noise

Every task's ground truth derives from the upstream Loghub corpus labels.
Those labels are not all equally trustworthy, and a serious consumer of the
benchmark should know where each family's GT comes from and what its known
weaknesses are. This page documents that, per dataset.

| Dataset | Label source | Granularity | Known issues |
|---|---|---|---|
| HDFS_v1 | `preprocessed/anomaly_label.csv` (575k labelled blocks; from Xu et al. 2009) | per-block | The block labels are known in the literature to contain a minority of mislabels/ambiguous traces; root-cause subtypes (`datanode_unreachable` vs `other`) are heuristic refinements by our adapter, not upstream gold. |
| Hadoop | `abnormal_label.txt` (55 applications) | per-application (coarse) | Gold fault injection (machine_down / disk_full / network_disconnect), but app-level: every line window from an abnormal app inherits the app's label, so some windows show mostly-normal activity. |
| BGL | inline 0th-column alert tag (`KERNDTLB`, `APPSEV`, …) | per-line | Highest-fidelity labels in the corpus (operator-assigned alert categories). The tag is visible in the log text — see the inline-label caveat in [scoring.md](scoring.md). |
| Thunderbird | inline 0th-column alert tag | per-line | Same fidelity as BGL, but heavily skewed: VAPI is ~99.6% of tagged anomalies, so root-cause diversity is structurally limited. |
| OpenStack | `anomaly_labels.txt` (4 anomalous VM UUIDs) | per-VM-instance | Only 4 anomalous instances exist, all the same injected fault (abrupt VM destruction) — root-cause classification on this dataset is constant by construction; scoring weights evidence, not the category. |

## Family-level notes

- **tmpl (template extraction)** ground truth derives from the published
  Loghub-2k structured CSVs (`<Dataset>_2k.log_structured.csv`). Those
  files — including their templates — are public since 2023 and are
  plausibly in LLM training data, and the task itself is solvable by a
  deterministic Drain-style templater. For both reasons the family is
  being moved out of the scored leaderboard set into an unscored tooling
  track (see CHANGELOG for v1.0).
- **rem (remediation)** is synthetic: topology, service state, configs
  and the fault→remedy mapping are generated by
  `tools/case_builder/remediation.py`; the *diagnosis* evidence still
  comes from real corpus log windows.
- **fp (false-positive triage)** windows are sampled from benign-but-noisy
  regions of the corpora; the v1.0 regeneration mixes in subtle true
  incidents so that `is_incident` is a real decision rather than a
  family constant.

## Contamination posture

- Every text surface carries a **canary GUID** (tripwire for verbatim
  training contamination).
- The verbatim-snippet and exact `(file, line)` requirements mean
  memorized corpus knowledge alone cannot pass evidence checks — the
  agent must read the actual (re-sliced, re-numbered) files shipped in
  the task.
- A **held-out, seed-varied stress pack** with private ground truth is
  planned for v1.0 (public sha256 commitments of the hidden
  `expected.json` files; see the roadmap in the v1.0 changelog).
